Chapter 1: Exploring Fauna of Indonesia

Satwa Endemik Indonesia, Teks Laporan Ilmiah (Report Text Structure), Tata Bahasa Kalimat Pasif (Passive Voice: is/are + V3), serta Pelestarian Hewan Langka.

📍 Navigasi Cepat: 1. Pengertian 2. Jenis 3. Kegunaan 4. Aturan 5. Perbedaan 6. Contoh 7. Kesalahan Umum 📝 Kuis

🎯 Tujuan Pembelajaran

Setelah mempelajari unit ini, santri diharapkan mampu:

  1. Mengidentifikasi kosakata nama hewan endemik Indonesia (Bekantan, Orangutan, Komodo, Harimau Sumatra, Cenderawasih).

  3. Menguasai penggunaan Passive Voice (is / are + Verb 3) untuk mendeskripsikan habitat dan fakta ilmiah hewan.
  4. Membedakan antara Report Text (fakta umum spesies) dan Descriptive Text (deskripsi spesifik).
  5. Menyusun laporan fakta ilmiah tentang fauna khas Nusantara dalam Bahasa Inggris.

📖 1. Pengertian

Exploring Fauna of Indonesia adalah topik pembelajaran yang membahas keanekaragaman satwa khas/endemik Nusantara melalui karya tulis ilmiah populer berupa Report Text.

Report Text adalah teks yang menyajikan informasi atau fakta ilmiah tentang suatu hal (hewan, tumbuhan, atau fenomena alam) secara umum berdasarkan hasil pengamatan atau penelitian.

Tata bahasa utama yang digunakan dalam teks laporan fakta ilmiah adalah Passive Voice (Kalimat Pasif), di mana fokus pembicaraan terletak pada objek/hewan yang dikenai tindakan (misal: "Bekantans are found in Borneo.").

📐 4. Aturan & Struktur

Bagian ini menguraikan rumus Passive Voice dan struktur generik Report Text.

4.1 Pola Kalimat Pasif (Passive Voice: is / are + Verb 3)

Bentuk Kalimat Pola Rumus Contoh Kalimat Pasif
Aktif (Active) Subject + Verb 1 + Object Poachers hunt endangered orangutans.
Pasif (Passive) Object + is / are + Verb 3 + by Subject Endangered orangutans are hunted by poachers.
Bekantans are found in Borneo.

4.2 Struktur Generik Report Text (Report Text Structure)

1. General Classification (Klasifikasi Umum): Pernyataan umum yang mengidentifikasi hewan/spesies yang dilaporkan.
Example: "The Bekantan or proboscis monkey is a reddish-brown arboreal primate endemic to Borneo."
2. Description (Deskripsi Detail): Menguraikan habitat, makanan, penampilan fisik, dan perilaku secara ilmiah.
Example: "They are found in mangrove forests. Leaves and fruits are eaten by Bekantans..."
Model Teks Laporan ilmiah Utuh

Bekantan: The Long-Nosed Primate of Borneo

The Bekantan, also known as the proboscis monkey, is an arboreal primate that is endemic to the island of Borneo. They are easily recognized by the male's unusually large and long nose.

Bekantans are found mainly in mangrove forests, coastal areas, and near rivers. Their reddish-brown fur covers most of their bodies. Leaves, seeds, and unripe fruits are eaten by Bekantans as their main diet. They are also excellent swimmers and are known to leap from trees into rivers. Unfortunately, Bekantans are threatened by habitat loss and deforestation.

💬 6. Contoh Kalimat

Berikut contoh kalimat laporan fakta hewan beserta terjemahan Bahasa Indonesia:

No Kalimat Bahasa Inggris Terjemahan Bahasa Indonesia Topik Terkait

2 Leaves and fruits are eaten by proboscis monkeys. Daun-daun dan buah-buahan dimakan oleh bekantan. Passive Voice (are eaten)
3 Komodo dragons are protected in National Parks. Komodo dilindungi di Taman Nasional. Passive Voice (are protected)

⚠️ 7. Kesalahan Umum

Berikut 3 kesalahan yang paling sering dilakukan santri pada materi Passive Voice & Report Text:

❌ Kesalahan 1: Menggunakan Verb 1/Verb 2 alih-alih Verb 3 pada Passive Voice

Salah: "Bekantans are find / founding in Borneo."
✅ Benar: "Bekantans are found (Verb 3) in Borneo."

💡 Pola kalimat pasif wajib memakai To Be + Verb 3 (is found, are eaten, is protected).

❌ Kesalahan 2: Salah memilih is atau are pada subjek jamak

Salah: "Leaves and fruits is eaten by Bekantans."
✅ Benar: "Leaves and fruits are eaten by Bekantans."
