Chapter 1: About Me

Memperkenalkan Diri & Orang Lain, Kata Ganti (Pronouns), To Be, Hobi, Frekuensi Kebiasaan, Ciri Fisik & Sifat, serta Menulis Teks Deskriptif.

📍 Navigasi Cepat: 1. Pengertian 2. Jenis 3. Kegunaan 4. Aturan 5. Perbedaan 6. Contoh 7. Kesalahan Umum 📝 Kuis

🎯 Tujuan Pembelajaran

Setelah mempelajari unit ini, santri diharapkan mampu:

  1. Memperkenalkan diri sendiri dan orang lain (self-introduction & introducing others) dalam Bahasa Inggris.
  2. Menggunakan kata ganti subjek (Subject Pronouns) dan kata ganti kepemilikan (Possessive Adjectives) dengan benar.
  3. Membentuk kalimat positif, negatif, dan tanya menggunakan To Be (am, is, are).
  4. Menyebutkan hobi dan peralatan yang dibutuhkan menggunakan pola like / enjoy + V-ing.
  5. Menggunakan kata keterangan frekuensi (Adverbs of Frequency) untuk menjelaskan kebiasaan.
  6. Mendeskripsikan ciri fisik dan sifat kepribadian seseorang, serta membedakan penggunaan is + Adjective dan has + Noun.
  7. Menyusun paragraf deskriptif (Descriptive Text) tentang teman sekelas.
💡 Catatan Guru (Teacher's Guide):
  • Siswa Visual: Manfaatkan tabel kosakata, bagan pronouns, dan teks percakapan terstruktur.
  • Siswa Auditori: Gunakan pemutar audio pelafalan dan minta santri menirukan intonasi (listen and repeat).
  • Siswa Kinestetik: Lakukan simulasi Classroom Survey Activity di mana santri berdiri dan mewawancarai teman sekelas.

📖 1. Pengertian

Self-Introduction (perkenalan diri) adalah kegiatan menyampaikan informasi dasar tentang diri sendiri kepada orang lain dalam Bahasa Inggris. Informasi yang disampaikan biasanya meliputi: nama lengkap (full name), nama panggilan (nickname), umur (age), asal kota (origin), dan alamat/tempat tinggal (address).

Selain memperkenalkan diri sendiri, kita juga perlu bisa memperkenalkan orang lain (introducing others) — misalnya memperkenalkan teman sekamar kepada teman baru.

Hasil akhir dari unit ini adalah santri mampu menyusun teks deskriptif (Descriptive Text) sederhana tentang seseorang, yang mencakup identitas, ciri fisik, sifat kepribadian, dan hobi.

📂 2. Jenis-Jenis Perkenalan

Jenis Perkenalan Penjelasan Contoh Ungkapan Kunci
Self-Introduction
(Perkenalan Diri Sendiri)		 Menyampaikan informasi tentang diri sendiri secara langsung. Menggunakan kata ganti I dan My. "My name is Fatimah. I am 12 years old."
Introducing Others
(Memperkenalkan Orang Lain)		 Menyampaikan informasi tentang orang lain kepada pihak ketiga. Menggunakan kata ganti She / He / They dan Her / His / Their. "This is my friend, Salma. She is from Kudus."

💡 3. Digunakan Untuk Apa?

Perkenalan diri dan kemampuan mendeskripsikan orang dipakai dalam situasi sehari-hari, misalnya:

  • 🏫 Hari pertama masuk pondok/sekolah baru — santri berkenalan dengan teman sekamar dan guru baru.
  • 🤝 Memperkenalkan teman baru — misalnya, santri senior memperkenalkan santri baru kepada yang lain.
  • ✍️ Mengisi formulir identitas — menulis nama, umur, asal, dan alamat dalam Bahasa Inggris.
  • 📝 Tugas menulis deskripsi teman — menyusun paragraf deskriptif tentang ciri fisik, sifat, dan hobi sahabat.
  • 🎤 Presentasi singkat di depan kelas — menceritakan tentang diri sendiri atau teman secara lisan.

📐 4. Aturan & Struktur

Bagian ini membahas pola-pola tata bahasa yang diperlukan untuk memperkenalkan diri, mendeskripsikan orang, dan menyusun teks deskriptif.

4.1 Ungkapan Perkenalan Diri (Self-Introduction Expressions)

Komponen Ungkapan (Expression) Pengucapan (Phonetic) Contoh Kalimat
Greetings Assalamu'alaikum / Good morning /ɡʊd ˈmɔːnɪŋ/ Assalamu'alaikum, Good morning everyone!
Full Name My name is... /maɪ neɪm ɪz/ My name is Fatimah Az-Zahra.
Nickname You can call me... /juː kæn kɔːl miː/ You can call me Fatimah.
Age I am ... years old. /aɪ æm ... jɪəz əʊld/ I am 12 years old.
Origin & Address I come from ... / I live in ... /aɪ kʌm frɒm/ /aɪ lɪv ɪn/ I come from Solo and I live in Dormitory A.

💬 Contoh Percakapan Perkenalan Diri:

"Assalamu'alaikum warahmatullah. Good morning, Ustadzah and friends. Let me introduce myself. My name is Aisha Humaira. You can call me Aisha. I am 13 years old. I come from Semarang. I live in Room 3, PPTQ Jamal Yusuf Al-Haddad. Nice to meet you all!"

4.2 Kata Ganti Subjek & Kepemilikan (Subject Pronouns & Possessive Adjectives)

Subject Pronoun (Subjek) Makna Possessive Adjective (Kepunyaan) Contoh Kalimat
I Saya My (Milik saya) I am Fatimah. My hobby is reading.
She Dia (Perempuan) Her (Miliknya) She is from Kudus. Her nickname is Salma.
He Dia (Laki-laki) His (Miliknya) He is our teacher. His house is in Tegal.
They Mereka Their (Milik mereka) They are friendly. Their room is clean.
We Kami / Kita Our (Milik kami) We study together. Our school is PPTQ.
You Kamu / Kalian Your (Milik kamu) You are kind. Your hijab is neat.
It Itu (benda/hewan) Its (Miliknya) It is a cat. Its fur is white.

💬 Contoh Percakapan Memperkenalkan Teman:

Fatimah: "Hi Maryam! Have you met my new roommate?"

Maryam: "No, I haven't. Who is she?"

Fatimah: "Maryam, this is my friend, Khadijah. She comes from Magelang. Her hobby is reading storybooks."

Maryam: "Hello Khadijah, nice to meet you!"

Khadijah: "Hello Maryam, nice to meet you too!"

4.3 Kata Kerja Bantu “To Be” (am, is, are)

Subjek To Be Kalimat Positif (+) Kalimat Negatif (-) Kalimat Tanya (?)
I am I am a santri. I am not late. Am I in Class 7A?
She / He / It is She is diligent. He is not lazy. Is she your roommate?
You / We / They are They are friendly. We are not tired. Are you from Solo?

4.4 Hobi & Peralatan (Hobbies & Tools)

Pola kalimat untuk menyatakan hobi: Subject + like / love / enjoy + V-ing.

Kategori Hobi Nama Hobi (Verb-ing) Alat (Tools) Contoh Kalimat
Olahraga (Sports) Playing badminton / Archery Racket, shuttlecock, bow, arrows Aisha likes playing badminton. She needs a racket.
Kesenian (Arts) Painting / Calligraphy (Khat) Canvas, paintbrushes, bamboo pen, ink Khadijah enjoys calligraphy. She uses a bamboo pen.
Literasi (Literacy) Reading storybooks / Writing Novel, storybooks, bookmark, notebook Maryam loves reading storybooks. She has a big bookshelf.
Kuliner (Culinary) Cooking / Baking cakes Stove, pan, apron, recipe book Fatimah likes cooking. She wears a clean apron.

4.5 Kata Keterangan Frekuensi (Adverbs of Frequency)

Digunakan untuk menyatakan seberapa sering suatu kebiasaan dilakukan. Posisinya: sebelum kata kerja utama, tetapi setelah To Be.

Adverb Persentase Contoh Kalimat Kebiasaan Santri
Always 100% (Selalu) I always read Al-Qur'an after Subuh prayer.
Usually 80% (Biasanya) Aisha usually plays badminton on Saturday.
Often 60% (Sering) We often borrow books from the library.
Sometimes 40% (Kadang) Sometimes, she bakes cookies with her friends.
Rarely 20% (Jarang) She rarely watches TV because there is none in the dorm.
Never 0% (Tidak pernah) He never forgets to bring his homework.

4.6 Ciri Fisik & Sifat Kepribadian (Physical & Personality Traits)

A. Aturan: “is + Adjective” vs “has + Noun”

Pola Jenis Kata Setelahnya Contoh
Subject + IS + ... Kata Sifat (Adjectives) She is tall. / She is kind and diligent.
Subject + HAS + ... Kata Benda / Fitur (Nouns) She has round brown eyes. / She has an oval face.

B. Daftar Sifat Kepribadian Positif

Kata Sifat (Adjective) Arti Deskripsi Perilaku
KindBaik hatiAlways helps friends warmly.
PoliteSopanGreets teachers respectfully.
DiligentRajin / TekunStudies hard and completes tasks on time.
CheerfulCeriaAlways smiles and is warm to everyone.
HumbleRendah hatiDoesn't boast and stays modest.
PatientSabarStays calm when facing difficulties.

4.7 Struktur Teks Deskriptif (Descriptive Text Structure)

Teks deskriptif digunakan untuk menggambarkan seseorang, tempat, atau benda secara rinci. Struktur generiknya terdiri dari 2 bagian:

1. Identification (Pengenalan): Mengenalkan nama, umur, asal kota, dan hubungan pertemanan.
Example: "My best friend in the dorm is Fatimah. She is 12 years old and comes from Solo."
2. Description (Deskripsi Rinci): Menguraikan ciri fisik, sifat kepribadian, hobi, dan frekuensi kebiasaan.
Example: "Fatimah is tall and wears a neat hijab. She is very kind and diligent. Her hobby is reading..."
Model Teks Deskriptif Utuh

My Best Friend, Fatimah

My best friend in PPTQ Jamal Yusuf Al-Haddad is Fatimah Az-Zahra. She is twelve years old and she comes from Solo, Central Java. We live in the same dormitory room and study together every day.

Fatimah is tall and slender. She has round brown eyes and always wears a neat white hijab. Fatimah is a very kind, polite, and diligent girl. She always greets everyone warmly and helps her friends when studying English. Her favorite hobby is reading Islamic storybooks. She always reads books before sleeping. I am very happy to have a best friend like Fatimah.

⚖️ 5. Perbedaan dengan Materi yang Mirip

Berikut adalah dua pasangan yang sering tertukar oleh pemula. Perhatikan perbedaannya:

A. Subject Pronoun vs Possessive Adjective

Subject Pronoun (Subjek Kalimat) Possessive Adjective (Kepemilikan) Contoh Benar
She → dipakai sebagai subjek Her → dipakai sebelum kata benda She is kind. Her name is Aisha.
He → dipakai sebagai subjek His → dipakai sebelum kata benda He is a teacher. His class is 7A.
They → dipakai sebagai subjek Their → dipakai sebelum kata benda They study together. Their room is clean.

❌ Salah: "Her is from Solo." → yang benar: "She is from Solo."

B. “is + Adjective” vs “has + Noun”

Pola Kapan Dipakai ✅ Contoh Benar ❌ Contoh Salah
is + Adjective Mendeskripsikan sifat/keadaan umum She is tall. She has tall.
has + Noun Mendeskripsikan fitur fisik spesifik yang dimiliki She has brown eyes. She is brown eyes.

Berikut contoh kalimat yang relevan dengan kehidupan santri, disertai terjemahan Bahasa Indonesia:

No Kalimat Bahasa Inggris Terjemahan Bahasa Indonesia Topik Terkait

⚠️ 7. Kesalahan Umum

Berikut 3 kesalahan yang paling sering dilakukan santri pemula terkait materi di unit ini:

❌ Kesalahan 1: Tertukar antara Subject Pronoun dan Possessive Adjective

Salah: "Her is from Solo and she hobby is reading."
✅ Benar: "She is from Solo and her hobby is reading."

💡 Ingat: She/He/They = subjek kalimat (pelaku), Her/His/Their = diikuti kata benda (kepemilikan).

❌ Kesalahan 2: Salah memilih “is” atau “has” saat mendeskripsikan fisik

Salah: "She has tall and is round brown eyes."
✅ Benar: "She is tall and has round brown eyes."

💡 Pakai is + kata sifat (tall, kind, cheerful). Pakai has + kata benda / fitur (brown eyes, oval face).

❌ Kesalahan 3: Salah meletakkan Adverb of Frequency

Salah: "Fatimah reads always books before sleeping."
✅ Benar: "Fatimah always reads books before sleeping."

💡 Adverb of frequency diletakkan sebelum kata kerja utama (always reads), tetapi setelah To Be (She is always kind).
