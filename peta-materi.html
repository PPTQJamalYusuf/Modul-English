Halaman ini khusus dirancang untuk memudahkan Guru dalam melihat Peta Sebaran Materi Inti & Tata Bahasa (Grammar Matrix) di setiap unit pembelajaran. Setiap kelas dipisah secara rapi sehingga Ustadzah dapat memetakan unsur bahasa (Nouns, Pronouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Adverbs, Prepositions, Grammar, dan Jenis Teks) sebelum mengajar.

📌 Peta Materi Kelas VII (Fase D - 5 Unit Pembelajaran)

Unit 1: Chapter 1 - About Me

Fokus: Memperkenalkan diri sendiri & orang lain, kata ganti, To Be, hobi, dan sifat fisik.

Kategori Bahasa Rincian Materi & Daftar Kosakata Spesifik
Grammar Utama To Be (am, is, are) • Subject Pronouns vs Possessive Adjectives
Pronouns (Kata Ganti) Subject: I, She, He, They, We, You, It
Possessive Adjectives: My, Her, His, Their, Our, Your, Its
Nouns (Kata Benda) Name, age, origin, address, dormitory, roommate, hobby, bow, arrows, racket, canvas, storybook, apron.
Verbs (Kata Kerja) Introduce, live, come, play, read, paint, cook, study, greet, help.
Adjectives (Kata Sifat) Kind, polite, diligent, cheerful, patient, humble, tall, round (eyes), oval (face).
Adverbs (Kata Keterangan) Adverbs of Frequency: Always (100%), Usually (80%), Often (60%), Sometimes (40%), Rarely (20%), Never (0%).
Jenis Teks Utama Descriptive Text (Mendeskripsikan ciri fisik & sifat sahabat/kamar pondok).
🚀 Buka Unit 1

Unit 2: Chapter 2 - Culinary and Me

Fokus: Makanan/minuman, rasa & tekstur, preferensi kuliner, takaran, dan resep prosedur.

Kategori Bahasa Rincian Materi & Daftar Kosakata Spesifik
Grammar Utama Countable vs Uncountable Nouns • Imperative Sentences (Verb 1) • Preferences (prefer A to B)
Nouns (Countable) Apple, banana, egg, lemon, plate, bowl, glass, cup, tablespoon, pinch, recipe.
Nouns (Uncountable) Rice, water, sugar, milk, oil, tea, coffee, salt, soup, honey, pepper.
Quantifiers (Takaran) A glass of, a cup of, a bowl of, a slice of, a plate of, a tablespoon of, a pinch of.
Verbs (Imperative V1) Cut, slice, pour, stir, add, squeeze, serve, brew, wash, mix.
Adjectives (Tastes/Textures) Sweet, savory, sour, spicy, salty, crispy, crunchy, soft, chewy, delicious.
Connectors (Urutan) First, Second, Next, Then, After that, Finally.
Jenis Teks Utama Procedural Text (Resep membuat makanan/minuman).

Unit 3: Chapter 3 - Home Sweet Home

Fokus: Ruangan kamar/pondok, perabotan, preposisi tempat, piket kebersihan, dan proyek daur ulang.

Kategori Bahasa Rincian Materi & Daftar Kosakata Spesifik
Grammar Utama There is / There are • Prepositions of Place • Simple Present Tense (Piket) • Imperatives
Nouns (Rooms & Items) Dormitory, study hall, living hall, washroom, bed, pillow, desk, wardrobe, locker, bookshelf, clock, broom, dustpan, mop.
Prepositions of Place In, on, under, next to, beside, behind, in front of, above.
Verbs (Chores Routines) Sweep (sweeps), mop (mops), wash (washes), tidy up (tidies up), make (makes), take out.
Jenis Teks Utama Descriptive Text & Instruction Guide (Deskripsi kamar & DIY recycling project).

Unit 4: Chapter 4 - My School Activities

Kategori Bahasa Rincian Materi & Daftar Kosakata Spesifik

Unit 5: Chapter 5 - This Is My School

Fokus: Fasilitas sekolah, meminta & memberi petunjuk arah, ekstrakurikuler, dan deskripsi sekolah.

📌 Peta Materi Kelas VIII (Fase D - 5 Unit Pembelajaran)

Unit 1: Chapter 1 - Celebrating School Events

Fokus: Pengalaman lomba 17-an, Simple Past Tense (V1->V2), time connectors, dan teks recount.

Kategori Bahasa Rincian Materi & Daftar Kosakata Spesifik
Grammar Utama Simple Past Tense (+ / - / ?) • Regular vs Irregular Verbs • Time Connectors
Verbs (Regular V1→V2) play→played, join→joined, celebrate→celebrated, watch→watched, listen→listened.
Verbs (Irregular V1→V2) go→went, win→won, eat→ate, see→saw, take→took, fall→fell, run→ran.
Nouns (Events) Independence Day, sack race, tug-of-war, marble-in-spoon, cracker eating, prize, medal.
Connectors (Time) First, First of all, Then, After that, Finally.
Jenis Teks Utama Personal Recount Text (Menceritakan pengalaman lomba pribadi).
